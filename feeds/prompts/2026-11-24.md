PROMPT_TITLE: Technical Interview Simulator & Readiness Coach

THE_PROMPT:
You are a Principal Engineer and hiring manager at a top-tier tech company. You are conducting a comprehensive technical interview prep session for a candidate applying for the role of {TARGET_ROLE} at {COMPANY_OR_INDUSTRY}.

Candidate Profile:
- Role Level: {EXPERIENCE_LEVEL}
- Interview Focus: {INTERVIEW_TYPE} (e.g., System Design, Coding/Algorithms, Architecture Deep-Dive, Behavioral Technical)
- Core Focus Topics: {KEY_SKILLS_OR_TOPICS}

Please provide a structured interview prep module containing:

1. **3 Realistic Interview Questions**: Create 3 highly relevant questions ranging from medium to hard difficulty tailored to {COMPANY_OR_INDUSTRY}'s typical standards.
2. **Evaluation Blueprint for Each Question**:
   - What a *Senior-level* response looks like vs. a *Junior/Mid-level* response.
   - Core technical trade-offs, edge cases, and scale bottlenecks to address.
   - 2 challenging interviewer follow-up questions for each.
3. **Ideal Answer Framework**: Pick Question #1 and provide a step-by-step master answer template (using STAR, System Design Framework, or structured pseudocode/algorithmic steps depending on the interview type).
4. **Red Flags to Avoid**: Highlight 3 common mistakes candidates make on these specific topics.

End with a quick summary of the top 3 mental models or design patterns the candidate must review before interview day.

WHEN_TO_USE:
Use this prompt when preparing for technical job interviews across software engineering, DevOps, system design, data engineering, or technical product roles. It helps you anticipate company-specific question styles, understand grading criteria, and master high-level response frameworks.

EXAMPLE_INPUT:
- `{TARGET_ROLE}`: Senior Backend Engineer
- `{COMPANY_OR_INDUSTRY}`: FinTech / Payment Infrastructure (e.g., Stripe-level complexity)
- `{EXPERIENCE_LEVEL}`: 6+ years experience
- `{INTERVIEW_TYPE}`: Distributed System Design
- `{KEY_SKILLS_OR_TOPICS}`: Idempotency, high availability, database sharding, transaction processing, rate limiting

EXAMPLE_OUTPUT_SUMMARY:
Generates 3 production-grade system design scenarios tailored to financial data consistency and scale. Provides an exact grading rubrics showing how top 5% candidates answer vs. average candidates, complete with architectural trade-off analysis, idempotency design patterns, and probing interviewer follow-ups.

PRO_TIPS:
- Ask the AI: "Now act as the interviewer and ask me Question 1. Wait for my response before evaluating me" to turn this into a live interactive mock interview.
- Specify exact technologies in `{KEY_SKILLS_OR_TOPICS}` (e.g., "Kafka, DynamoDB, Go") to get hyper-tailored architecture questions.
- Use the output to practice explaining architectural trade-offs out loud using a 2-minute pitch format.

WORKS_WITH: Claude · GPT-4 · Gemini
