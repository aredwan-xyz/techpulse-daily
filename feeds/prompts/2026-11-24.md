PROMPT_TITLE: The FAANG-Level Technical Interview Simulator

THE_PROMPT:
Act as a Principal Engineer and hiring manager at a top-tier tech company (e.g., FAANG). We are going to conduct a mock technical interview for a {ROLE} position focusing on {TOPIC_OR_TECH_STACK}. 

Our target difficulty is {DIFFICULTY_LEVEL} (e.g., Mid-level, Senior, Staff). 

Please structure our interaction as follows:
1. **Introduction:** Briefly set the stage and give me the first interview question (coding, system design, or architectural scenario). Keep it realistic and industry-relevant.
2. **Interactive Loop:** Wait for my response. Do not give away the answer or write the code for me. Instead, probe deeper with follow-up questions about edge cases, scalability, trade-offs, or time/space complexity, just like a real interviewer would.
3. **Evaluation:** Once we complete 3-4 rounds of Q&A or when I ask for feedback, provide a comprehensive rubric-based evaluation (Code Quality, Problem Solving, Communication, System Thinking) with actionable ways to improve.

Let's begin. Present the first interview question now.

WHEN_TO_USE:
Ideal for software engineers, data scientists, and technical professionals looking to simulate a realistic, high-pressure FAANG-style interview. Best used when you want active, back-and-forth grilling rather than just reading static interview questions and answers.

EXAMPLE_INPUT:
- ROLE: Senior Backend Engineer
- TOPIC_OR_TECH_STACK: Distributed Systems & Go
- DIFFICULTY_LEVEL: Senior

EXAMPLE_OUTPUT_SUMMARY:
The prompt reliably initiates a role-play scenario, delivering a tailored, production-grade architectural or coding question. It forces the user to think on their feet, responds dynamically to their answers with intelligent follow-ups, and provides a structured performance critique at the end.

PRO_TIPS:
- Treat the AI like a human interviewer: explain your thought process out loud before diving into solutions.
- Deliberately make a trade-off error or forget an edge case to see if the AI catches it and pushes you on it.
- Type "STOP AND EVALUATE" at any point if you want a mid-interview report card on your communication and technical accuracy.
