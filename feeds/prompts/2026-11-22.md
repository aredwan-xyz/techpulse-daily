PROMPT_TITLE: Executive Data Insights & Action Engine

THE_PROMPT:
Act as a Senior Data Strategist and BI Analyst. Analyze the provided dataset/metrics to extract high-leverage business insights, spot anomalies, and deliver clear recommendations.

Context: {BUSINESS_CONTEXT}
Primary Goal: {PRIMARY_GOAL}
Target Audience: {TARGET_AUDIENCE}

Data to Analyze:
```
{DATA_SET}
```

Perform your analysis and structure your output exactly as follows:

1. **Executive Summary (BLUF)**: Provide a 2-3 sentence overview of the macro trends and primary takeaway.
2. **Key Insights & Pattern Analysis**: List 3-5 significant insights, anomalies, or trends. Every point MUST cite specific numbers, percentages, or metrics directly from the data.
3. **Prioritized Action Plan**: Provide 3 concrete, strategic actions based on the findings. Format each as:
   - **Action Item**: [Specific step to take]
   - **Expected Impact vs. Effort**: [High/Med/Low Impact | High/Med/Low Effort]
   - **Target Metric**: [Which KPI this will move]
4. **Data Caveats & Next Steps**: Identify 2 potential data blind spots/biases and 2 follow-up metrics to investigate next.

Maintain an analytical, concise, and business-focused tone.

WHEN_TO_USE:
Use this prompt when you have raw CSV data, KPI reports, survey results, or performance dashboards and need to turn numbers into strategic business insights quickly. It works best when prepping executive briefings, quarterly business reviews (QBRs), or operational retrospectives.

EXAMPLE_INPUT:
BUSINESS_CONTEXT: Q3 E-commerce Sales & Marketing Performance for a B2C SaaS Tool.
PRIMARY_GOAL: Identify why CAC increased while conversion rates dropped in August, and recommend fixes for Q4.
TARGET_AUDIENCE: VP of Growth & CMO

DATA_SET:
Month, Ad Spend, Clicks, Signups, Paid Conversions, CAC ($), Conv Rate (%)
July, $50,000, 100,000, 5,000, 500, $100, 10%
August, $85,000, 140,000, 5,600, 392, $216, 7%
September, $70,000, 115,000, 4,800, 432, $162, 9%

EXAMPLE_OUTPUT_SUMMARY:
The response provides a sharp executive summary showing that August's 70% ad spend increase yielded diminishing returns due to poor top-of-funnel traffic quality. It breaks down exact conversion drops, highlights CAC escalation metrics ($100 to $216), and delivers 3 prioritized Q4 strategies (retargeting reallocation, landing page CRO, budget capping) complete with impact/effort ratings.

PRO_TIPS:
- Paste data in clean CSV or Markdown table formats for the most accurate quantitative parsing.
- If working with large datasets, summarize key aggregates (totals, averages, MoM changes) before feeding them into the prompt.
- Customize the `{TARGET_AUDIENCE}` tag (e.g., "CFO" vs. "Engineering Manager") to automatically adjust the tone and metric focus.

WORKS_WITH: Claude · GPT-4 · Gemini
