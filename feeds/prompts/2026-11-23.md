PROMPT_TITLE: High-Impact Technical Email Architect

THE_PROMPT:
Act as a Principal Engineer and Technical Communications Lead. Write a professional, polished technical email based on the parameters below. 

Your goal is to convey complex technical information clearly, respect the reader's time, and drive a specific action or decision.

INPUT PARAMETERS:
- **Recipient:** {RECIPIENT_ROLE} (e.g., Engineering Manager, CISO, Product Owner, External Vendor)
- **Primary Objective:** {PRIMARY_OBJECTIVE} (e.g., Request architectural approval, report a major incident post-mortem, escalate a blocker, explain a technical delay)
- **Key Technical Details:** {TECHNICAL_DETAILS} (e.g., API latency spiked by 400%, database migration requires 2-hour downtime, refactoring legacy auth module)
- **Tone:** {TONE} (e.g., Urgent, Collaborative, Direct, Informative)
- **Desired Call to Action (CTA):** {CALL_TO_ACTION} (e.g., Approve budget, review pull request by Tuesday, attend sync meeting)

EMAIL STRUCTURE TO FOLLOW:
1. **Subject Line:** Action-oriented, prefixed with context (e.g., `[ACTION REQUIRED]`, `[UPDATE]`, `[INCIDENT]`), and under 60 characters.
2. **The Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF):** 1-2 sentences stating why you are emailing and what is needed.
3. **Context / Technical Breakdown:** Bullet points explaining the "what" and "why" without drowning the reader in unnecessary jargon.
4. **Impact / Risk:** What happens if we do (or don't) act?
5. **Next Steps & CTA:** Clear, bolded instructions with a deadline if applicable.

WHEN_TO_USE:
Ideal for writing high-stakes engineering emails, communicating complex architectural decisions to non-technical stakeholders, or delivering incident reports and project updates that require clear accountability and action.

EXAMPLE_INPUT:
- **Recipient:** Engineering Director
- **Primary Objective:** Request approval for an emergency database migration
- **Key Technical Details:** Postgres primary DB running out of disk space due to unindexed audit logs. Need to migrate to a larger instance and run cleanup scripts during a 30-minute maintenance window tonight.
- **Tone:** Urgent but controlled
- **Call_to_action:** Approve the emergency maintenance window by 4:00 PM today

EXAMPLE_OUTPUT_SUMMARY:
Produces a crisp, scannable email featuring a clear urgency-tagged subject line, a BLUF summary paragraph, bulleted technical context, and a prominent call-to-action that ensures rapid stakeholder sign-off.

PRO_TIPS:
- Always specify the exact deadline in the placeholder if the email requires a decision, to prevent vague "ASAP" bottlenecks.
- If writing for a mixed technical/non-technical audience, use the `TECHNICAL_DETAILS` variable to paste raw logs or metrics, and ask the prompt to "translate complex jargon into business risk."
- Keep the `TONE` strictly professional; even in urgent scenarios, maintaining a calm, solution-oriented voice yields faster approvals.

WORKS_WITH: Claude · GPT-4 · Gemini
