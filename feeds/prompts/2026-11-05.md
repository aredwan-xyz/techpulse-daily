PROMPT_TITLE: AI Enterprise System Architect

THE_PROMPT:
Act as a Principal Enterprise System Architect with 20+ years of experience designing hyper-scalable, fault-tolerant distributed systems. 

Design a production-ready architecture for {SYSTEM_NAME}, which requires handling {DAILY_ACTIVE_USERS} daily active users, a peak load of {PEAK_REQUESTS_PER_SECOND} requests per second, and {DATA_VOLUME_GROWTH} of data growth per month. The primary tech stack constraint is {TECH_STACK_CONSTRAINTS}.

Provide your response in the following structured format:
1. **High-Level Architecture & Data Flow**: Describe the core components (ingress, compute, caching, storage) and how data flows through the system.
2. **Component Breakdown & Technology Choices**: Recommend specific technologies for API gateway, load balancing, message queues, databases, and caching layers with justifications.
3. **Scalability & High Availability Strategy**: Detail horizontal scaling, sharding/partitioning strategies, multi-region failover, and disaster recovery.
4. **Bottlenecks & Mitigation**: Identify 3 critical system bottlenecks and concrete engineering solutions for each.
5. **Observability & Security**: Outline the monitoring, logging, tracing, and zero-trust security measures required.

WHEN_TO_USE:
Ideal for software architects, tech leads, and senior developers who need to design, scale, or review complex distributed systems from scratch while accounting for performance bottlenecks and high-availability requirements.

EXAMPLE_INPUT:
SYSTEM_NAME: Global Real-Time Collaborative Video Annotation Platform
DAILY_ACTIVE_USERS: 5,000,000
PEAK_REQUESTS_PER_SECOND: 80,000
DATA_VOLUME_GROWTH: 50 TB/month
TECH_STACK_CONSTRAINTS: Cloud-agnostic, Kubernetes-native, prefer open-source

EXAMPLE_OUTPUT_SUMMARY:
Produces a comprehensive, engineering-grade system design document complete with a component architecture overview, exact tech stack selections, scaling strategies for sudden traffic spikes, and proactive identification of data bottlenecks.

PRO_TIPS:
- [Tip 1] Specify exact regulatory requirements (like GDPR or HIPAA) in the constraints if compliance impacts your data storage design.
- [Tip 2] Use the bottleneck section to prompt the AI for edge-case failure modes specific to your domain.
- [Tip 3] Follow up by asking the AI to generate a C4 model component diagram or Terraform blueprint based on the architecture.

WORKS_WITH: Claude · GPT-4 · Gemini
