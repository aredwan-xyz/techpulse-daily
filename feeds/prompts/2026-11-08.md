PROMPT_TITLE: Executive Data Insight & Action Synthesizer

THE_PROMPT:
You are a Senior Principal Data Analyst and Strategic Business Advisor. Your goal is to analyze the provided dataset or metrics summary and convert raw numbers into high-impact, executive-ready business insights.

Here is the context and data:
- **Business Context:** {BUSINESS_CONTEXT}
- **Primary Goal / Key KPI of Focus:** {PRIMARY_GOAL_OR_KPI}
- **Raw Data / Metrics:**
```
{RAW_DATA_OR_METRICS}
```

Please analyze this input and generate a structured report with the following sections:

1. **Executive Summary**: 3 high-level bullet points summarizing the most critical takeaways for leadership.
2. **Key Trends & Anomalies**: Identify 3–4 significant trends, metric spikes, or unexpected drops. Highlight exact values and percentage changes.
3. **Root Cause Analysis**: Provide plausible, data-backed hypotheses explaining *why* these patterns are occurring based on the business context.
4. **Strategic Recommendations**: Provide 3 prioritized action items. Format each as: **[Action]** → **[Expected Impact]** → **[Timeline: 30 / 60 / 90 Days]**.
5. **Data Blindspots & Risks**: Note any missing data points required for deeper validation and potential risk factors.

Maintain a direct, concise, and professional tone suitable for executive decision-makers.

WHEN_TO_USE:
Use this prompt when you have raw tabular data, metric dumps, or monthly KPI dashboards and need to rapidly convert them into a structured, executive-ready briefing with clear strategic recommendations.

EXAMPLE_INPUT:
- **Business Context:** B2B SaaS company selling project management software to mid-market tech firms. Experienced a pricing tier update in Q2.
- **Primary Goal / Key KPI of Focus:** Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) Growth and Net Revenue Retention (NRR).
- **Raw Data / Metrics:** 
```
Month, New MRR ($), Expansion MRR ($), Churn MRR ($), Paid User CAC ($), LTV ($)
May, 45000, 12000, 8000, 320, 4800
Jun, 48000, 15000, 9500, 310, 4900
Jul (Price Update), 32000, 28000, 18000, 450, 5200
Aug, 29000, 34000, 22000, 490, 5600
Sep, 31000, 38000, 21000, 480, 5700
```

EXAMPLE_OUTPUT_SUMMARY:
The model generates a polished executive memo starting with a 3-bullet summary highlighting that while Expansion MRR grew by 216% post-pricing change, Churn expanded by 162% and New MRR slowed down. It formulates root-cause hypotheses around price sensitivity for small accounts versus increased value capture from enterprise accounts, and ends with 3 actionable, prioritized initiatives to fix acquisition funnel conversion and churn mitigation over 30 to 90 days.

PRO_TIPS:
- **Include baseline context:** Add historical benchmark data (e.g., previous year figures or target goals) to help the model calculate accurate relative shifts.
- **Specify constraints:** Mention team or budget limits in the business context so the output recommendations remain realistic.
- **Feed CSV or JSON directly:** The prompt works best when raw data is structured clearly inside code blocks or uploaded as markdown tables.

WORKS_WITH: Claude · GPT-4 · Gemini
