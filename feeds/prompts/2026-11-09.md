PROMPT_TITLE: The Technical Email Synthesizer

THE_PROMPT:
You are an expert technical communicator who excels at translating complex engineering concepts into clear, actionable, professional emails tailored to specific audiences.

Draft a professional email using the following input parameters:

- Audience: {AUDIENCE} (e.g., Executive Leadership, Non-technical Product Manager, Client, Internal Engineering Team)
- Primary Goal: {PRIMARY_GOAL} (e.g., Post-Mortem Incident Report, API Deprecation Notice, System Upgrade Request)
- Tone: {TONE} (e.g., Urgent yet reassuring, Formal, Direct, Collaborative)
- Raw Technical Notes: 
{RAW_TECHNICAL_NOTES}
- Action Required from Recipient: {ACTION_REQUIRED}
- Key Deadlines / Timeline: {DEADLINES}

Guidelines for the output:
1. Provide 2-3 subject line options (varying from direct to context-rich).
2. Begin with a 1-2 sentence "TL;DR" summary focused on business impact or immediate key outcomes.
3. Translate dense technical details into terms appropriate for the specified target audience without losing essential accuracy.
4. Structure the body using clear headers, bullet points, and bold text for critical metrics, risks, or dates.
5. End with an explicit Call-To-Action (CTA) clearly stating next steps, ownership, and deadlines.

WHEN_TO_USE:
Use this prompt when you need to send technical updates, outage reports, architecture change proposals, or product deprecation notices to stakeholders. It is especially useful when translating dense engineering notes into clean communications for business leaders or external clients.

EXAMPLE_INPUT:
- Audience: Non-technical Executive Leadership (VP of Product, COO)
- Primary Goal: Update on Redis cluster memory exhaustion outage and long-term fix proposal
- Tone: Professional, reassuring, and data-driven
- Raw Technical Notes: 
- 08:14 UTC: Redis primary node ran out of RAM (OOM killed) due to unindexed cache key buildup from the new search service.
- Caused 14 minutes of checkout failures (402 errors on /v2/checkout endpoint).
- Failover to replica triggered at 08:28 UTC.
- Hotfix deployed: Flush cache key pattern `search:temp:*` and cap TTL to 3600s.
- Need $2,500/mo budget increase to upgrade to Managed Redis Cluster with auto-scaling to prevent recurrence.
- Action Required: Approval for infrastructure budget increase.
- Key Deadlines / Timeline: Approval needed by Friday end-of-day.

EXAMPLE_OUTPUT_SUMMARY:
The prompt produces a structured email featuring multiple subject line choices, an executive TL;DR highlighting the 14-minute checkout impact and resolution, a non-jargon explanation of why the crash occurred, a bulleted breakdown of the permanent fix, and a clear budget approval request section complete with a Friday deadline.

PRO_TIPS:
- Paste raw, messy slack messages or incident terminal logs directly into `{RAW_TECHNICAL_NOTES}`—the prompt is designed to organize chaos.
- Explicitly set `{AUDIENCE}` to "Non-technical executive" if you want the model to convert technical terms (like OOM, TTL, Redis) into plain business impact language.
- Request an additional "Technical Appendix" section if you want to include deep technical specs at the bottom of the email for engineering recipients.

WORKS_WITH: Claude · GPT-4 · Gemini
