PROMPT_TITLE: Crafting Clear Product Requirements

THE_PROMPT:
You are tasked with writing a comprehensive product requirements document for {PRODUCT_NAME}. This document should encompass the following sections: 
1. **Overview**: Provide a brief description of the product's purpose, target audience, and key features.
2. **Functional Requirements**: List all essential functions the product must perform, detailing user interactions and system operations clearly.
3. **Non-Functional Requirements**: Outline performance, security, and usability requirements that are essential for the product’s success.
4. **Acceptance Criteria**: Define measurable criteria that the product must meet to be considered complete and acceptable.
5. **Assumptions and Dependencies**: State any assumptions that are being made and any dependencies that the product might have.

Make sure to include examples or scenarios where necessary to clarify complex points and facilitate better understanding.

WHEN_TO_USE:
This prompt is ideal for product managers, development teams, or stakeholders looking to document clear and structured product specifications that will guide the development process effectively.

EXAMPLE_INPUT:
PRODUCT_NAME: "Smart Home Assistant"

EXAMPLE_OUTPUT_SUMMARY:
This prompt produces a detailed product requirements document that clearly outlines the purpose, functionalities, performance expectations, and criteria for acceptance of the "Smart Home Assistant." The structured format ensures that all important information is included, making it easy for teams to understand what is needed for successful development.

PRO_TIPS:
- Provide as much context as possible when describing the product to ensure clarity in requirements.
- Be specific in the functional requirements section to prevent ambiguity during development.
- Encourage feedback from key stakeholders while drafting to ensure all perspectives are captured early on.
