PROMPT_TITLE: Technical Architecture Trade-Off Evaluator

THE_PROMPT:
You are acting as a Senior Principal Systems Architect. Evaluate a technical decision by analyzing trade-offs across competing architectural options.

### Context & Inputs
- **Problem Statement:** {PROBLEM_STATEMENT}
- **Proposed Options:** {PROPOSED_OPTIONS}
- **System Constraints & Scale:** {SYSTEM_CONSTRAINTS}
- **Team & Business Context:** {TEAM_CONTEXT}
- **Priority Criteria:** {DECISION_CRITERIA}

### Architectural Analysis Framework
Provide a structured evaluation covering:

1. **Trade-Off Matrix Table:** Compare options across: Cost (Infra & Ops), Complexity, Scalability, Time-to-Market, Developer Experience, Operational Burden, and Reversibility (Two-Way vs. One-Way door). Use a 1-5 rating scale with concise justifications.
2. **Deep-Dive Trade-Off Analysis:** For each option, highlight:
   - What we GAIN (Immediate & long-term benefits)
   - What we SACRIFICE (Technical debt, vendor lock-in, complexity tax)
   - Edge cases & Failure Modes
3. **Reversibility & Migration Risk:** How hard is it to un-make this decision in 12-18 months if assumptions change?
4. **Final Recommendation (ADR Summary):** 
   - Recommended Option
   - Key Drivers for Recommendation
   - Top 3 Risk Mitigation Strategies for the chosen path

Be objective, call out hidden costs (e.g., cognitive load, ops overhead), and avoid buzzword bias.

WHEN_TO_USE:
Use this prompt when choosing between competing technology stacks, infrastructure patterns (e.g., Serverless vs. Containers), or architectural styles (e.g., Event-Driven vs. REST, Monolith vs. Microservices). It is ideal for drafting formal Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) before committing team engineering hours.

EXAMPLE_INPUT:
- **Problem Statement:** We need to rebuild our core notification engine to handle scheduled, transactional, and event-based pushes/emails at high volume.
- **Proposed Options:** Option A: Managed AWS Step Functions + SQS + Lambda; Option B: Self-hosted Temporal.io cluster on Kubernetes; Option C: Modular monolith using Postgres-backed background jobs (BullMQ).
- **System Constraints & Scale:** Peak throughput: 15,000 notifications/sec. Latency ceiling: <2 seconds. Budget: <$3k/month infra spend.
- **Team & Business Context:** 6 full-stack engineers, no dedicated DevOps/Kubernetes expert. High push to ship within 6 weeks.
- **Priority Criteria:** Time-to-market, Operational simplicity, Cost predictability.

EXAMPLE_OUTPUT_SUMMARY:
The model generates a comprehensive architectural decision document featuring a scored matrix table, an explicit breakdown of hidden operational costs vs. development speed, a reversibility assessment, and a definitive recommendation (e.g., favoring Option A for short-term velocity while outlining a migration path if scale outgrows budget limits).

PRO_TIPS:
- Include team headcount and specialized skills (or lack thereof) in `{TEAM_CONTEXT}`; architectural decisions fail more often due to team skills mismatch than raw technical limits.
- If you have strict latency or SLA targets, quantify them in `{SYSTEM_CONSTRAINTS}` to get more accurate failure mode analysis.
- Ask the model to explicitly flag "One-Way Door" (irreversible) decisions vs. "Two-Way Door" (easily reversible) choices in the summary.
