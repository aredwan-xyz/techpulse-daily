### 🎓 [Carnegie Mellon is putting computer science courses on YouTube for free](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/learn/)
> **Why it matters:** CMU opening its curriculum removes one of the last remaining moats of elite formal CS education. For software engineers and AI builders, this provides a world-class, zero-cost pipeline for rigorous fundamentals that bootcamps and quick-fix tutorials consistently miss.

### 🔒 [Optimizely compromised, served malware on Zoom, GitLab](https://blog.report-uri.com/worlds-largest-brands-infected-with-malware-via-optimizely/)
> **Why it matters:** This incident highlights the devastating risk of third-party JavaScript dependencies in modern web architecture. When a trusted experimentation platform is weaponized, even the most security-conscious tech giants become vectors for supply chain attacks against their own users.

### 🖼️ [Frame – 31.5" color ePaper display](https://trmnl.com/blog/introducing-frame)
> **Why it matters:** Large-format color ePaper is finally crossing the threshold from commercial novelty to viable developer hardware. It unlocks a new class of low-power, zero-glare ambient dashboards and local monitoring tools that won't burn out retinas or rack up excessive electricity bills.

### 🚪 [BugMeNot has closed](https://www.bugmenot.com/)
> **Why it matters:** The death of the internet's favorite credential-sharing workaround marks the end of an era for casual web scraping and frictionless reading. As paywalls and aggressive tracking harden, accessing basic information requires navigating an increasingly hostile, walled-garden web.

### 📜 [VisualBasic.World: A gallery of Planet Source Code programs from 1997 to 2012](https://visualbasic.world/)
> **Why it matters:** Preserving this digital archaeology isn't just about nostalgia; it’s a masterclass in how a generation of developers learned to build software before GitHub, Stack Overflow, and AI copilots existed. It reminds us that massive business logic was once powered entirely by spaghetti code, copy-pasted snippets, and raw determination.

*Editor's Take: Today's tech landscape is caught in a tug-of-war between open-source democratization—like CMU's curriculum and retro code preservation—and tightening structural walls, from third-party supply chain exploits to the final closing of open-web workarounds.*
