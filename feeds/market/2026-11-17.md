## 🔥 Hottest Skills Right Now

1. **Agentic AI Orchestration (LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen)**  
   The market has moved past simple API calls. Employers are actively seeking engineers who can design, evaluate, and debug non-deterministic multi-agent systems and tool-calling workflows.
2. **Rust (AI Infrastructure & High-Performance Microservices)**  
   As companies optimize inference costs and runtime performance, Rust expertise is heavily requested for compute-bound infrastructure and native web extensions.
3. **Triton / CUDA & Hardware-Level Optimization**  
   Engineers who can optimize model kernels and squeeze higher throughput out of limited GPU allocations remain among the most competitive candidates.
4. **TypeScript & Generative UI (Next.js, Tailwind, React Server Components)**  
   Full-stack roles demand mastery of real-time streaming interfaces, optimistic rendering, and dynamic AI-generated UI components.
5. **Hybrid Search & Vector DB Ops (pgvector, Qdrant, Milvus)**  
   Basic RAG is standard baseline; high-demand engineers understand advanced re-ranking, domain-specific chunking strategies, and low-latency vector search at scale.
