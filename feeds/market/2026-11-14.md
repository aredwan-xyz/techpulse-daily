# Daily Tech Job Market Pulse | November 14, 2026

Welcome to today’s tech talent market update. As we head into mid-November, the hiring landscape shows a distinct shift: companies have moved past the initial AI novelty phase and are now aggressively hiring developers who can build **reliable, low-latency, and cost-effective production systems**.

Here is today’s state of the engineering job market.

---

## 🔥 Hottest Skills Right Now

1. **AI Agent Architecture & MCP (Model Context Protocol)**  
    demand is surging for developers who know how to construct multi-agent orchestration loops, design reliable tool-calling systems, and implement standard context-sharing protocols.

2. **Inference Optimization & GPU Cluster Ops (vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, Ray)**  
   With inference costs dominating cloud expenses, engineers who can squeeze high throughput out of self-hosted clusters or optimize quantization (AWQ/FP8) are high-priority hires.

3. **Rust for AI Backends & Edge Systems**  
   Rust continues its domain expansion. From low-level AI runtime engines to high-concurrency gateway services, Rust fluency is yielding strong callback rates in systems engineering roles.

4. **TypeScript & Modern Async Frameworks (Next.js/Svelte/FastAPI)**  
   Full-stack developers with deep expertise in managing real-time web streams, dynamic UI states, and complex Client-AI workflows are heavily favored over traditional backend-only CRUD developers.

5. **LLM Evals & Synthetic Data Pipeline Curation**  
   Companies need developers who can write robust automated evaluation suites (Ragas, DeepEval, custom harness testing) to measure model drift and accuracy before shipping updates to production.
