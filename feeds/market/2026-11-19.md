# Tech Job Market Pulse: November 19, 2026

The "Who is hiring" sentiment this month shows a clear pivot: the gold rush for "LLM wrappers" has evaporated, replaced by a desperate, high-stakes search for **AI infrastructure architects** and **AI safety compliance engineers**. Companies are no longer asking "Can you use an API?" but rather "Can you optimize inference latency for a proprietary model?"

---

## 🔥 Hottest Skills Right Now
1.  **AI Orchestration (LangGraph, CrewAI):** Companies are moving past single-prompt scripts to complex, multi-agent workflows.
2.  **Vector Database Optimization (Pinecone/Milvus):** Expertise in RAG architectures at scale is the primary bottleneck for enterprise AI adoption.
3.  **Rust (for High-Performance Computing):** As energy costs for inference bite, companies are migrating heavy processing layers from Python to Rust.
4.  **Edge AI/On-Device Deployment:** Experience with ONNX, TensorRT, and quantized model deployment to local hardware.
5.  **Cloud FinOps for AI:** Specialized knowledge in managing skyrocketing GPU cloud spend is now a high-priority skill for CTOs.

---

## 💰 Salary Signals
*   **AI Infrastructure Engineers:** Salaries for mid-to-senior roles have spiked 12% YoY, with total compensation (TC) in the $220k–$310k range. 
*   **Full-Stack/AI Hybrid:** Developers who can bridge the gap between legacy React frontends and modern agentic backends are seeing a 15% salary premium over traditional full-stack roles.
*   **Equity vs. Cash:** In the current high-interest environment, candidates are increasingly negotiating for 15–20% higher base salaries rather than speculative equity options in mid-stage startups.

---

## 🚀 Roles in High Demand
1.  **AI Reliability Engineer:** Companies are terrified of hallucination-related PR disasters; these roles focus on testing, evaluation, and guardrail implementation.
2.  **Platform Engineer:** As companies consolidate their "AI sprawl," they need internal infrastructure experts to build secure, repeatable developer platforms.
3.  **Data Curation/Data Engineering Specialist:** With the "data wall" approaching for frontier models, engineers who can clean, synthesize, and manage high-quality training datasets are being poached aggressively.

---

## 📉 What's Cooling Off
*   **Generic Data Science/Analytics:** Roles that focus solely on descriptive analytics or reporting are seeing significant layoffs. If you aren't doing *generative* or *predictive* AI, you are in the "maintenance" bucket, which is shrinking.
*   **Junior "Prompt Engineering" Roles:** These have been largely absorbed into the responsibilities of standard software engineers.

---

## 🌍 Remote Landscape
The "Hybrid Mandate" has reached its saturation point. We are observing a bifurcation: 
*   **Big Tech:** Largely settled into 3-days-in-office mandates. 
*   **Series A/B Startups:** Moving toward "Remote-First with Quarterly Offsites" to capture elite talent that refuses to move to SF/NY. Remote-first remains the strongest weapon for mid-sized companies competing against FAANG for senior talent.

---

## 💡 Career Move of the Week
**Stop learning frameworks and start learning "Systems Thinking for AI."** Don’t just learn how to call an OpenAI API. Build a system that manages its own state, handles cost-tracking, includes a retry-logic for agent failures, and is fully observability-tracked. Recruiters are no longer looking for "OpenAI" on the resume—they are looking for your ability to build production-grade, fault-tolerant AI applications.
