## 🔥 Hottest Skills Right Now

1. **Agentic Frameworks (LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen)**
   The industry has moved well past simple wrapper chat interfaces. Companies are actively hiring engineers who can build, audit, and maintain reliable multi-agent workflows capable of autonomous task execution.
2. **Rust & C++ for Inference Optimization**
   With cloud costs under scrutiny, there’s a major surge in demand for developers who can optimize open-weights models at the hardware level using vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, and custom C++/Rust extensions.
3. **Graph Databases & GraphRAG (Neo4j, Memgraph)**
   Basic vector-only search is hitting precision bottlenecks in complex enterprise systems. Knowledge graphs integrated with vector embeddings are becoming the standard for enterprise-grade retrieval.
4. **GPU Cluster Orchestration (Ray, Kubernetes, Slurm)**
   Managing heterogeneous GPU clusters, dynamic dynamic scheduling, and minimizing idle GPU time is one of the highest-paid infra problems in late 2026.
5. **TypeScript & Modern Web Stack (Next.js 15+, Tailwind)**
   Full-stack product engineers who can build high-frequency real-time interfaces (WebSockets, streaming UI for AI model outputs) remain in consistent demand.
