## 🔥 Hottest Skills Right Now

1. **Agentic Orchestration Frameworks (LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen)**  
   Simple RAG implementations are now baseline. Companies are aggressively hiring for engineers who can orchestrate multi-agent workflows with state persistence, tool-use error handling, and deterministic fallback loops.

2. **Rust (AI Infrastructure & Systems)**  
   As teams push to lower inference costs and decrease tail latency, Rust is dominating the rewritten data pipelines, custom tokenizers, and high-performance inference engine layer.

3. **On-Device & Edge AI (ONNX Runtime, CoreML, GGML)**  
   Driven by privacy mandates and bandwidth costs, demand is surging for devs who can quantize, prune, and run 1B–7B parameter models efficiently on mobile and desktop clients.

4. **TypeScript / Next.js (App Router)**  
   Despite shifts in backend tech, the TypeScript + Next.js stack remains the indisputable default for high-velocity front-end and full-stack product development in modern startup stacks.

5. **Ray & Kubernetes for GPU Cluster Management**  
   Cost optimization is the defining metric for mature AI teams. Experience with distributed training/inference scheduling on raw K8s clusters or Ray is yielding immediate interview invites.
