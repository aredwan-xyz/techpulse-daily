# Tech Job Market Pulse: November 5, 2026

Data extracted from tech hiring signals, industry compensation benchmarks, and early analysis of this month's **"Ask HN: Who is hiring right now?"** thread.

---

## 🔥 Hottest Skills Right Now

1. **Agentic Frameworks & Multi-Agent Orchestration (LangGraph, AutoGen, Custom Rust Agents)**  
   Companies have moved past simple RAG architectures. Demand is spiking for engineers who can orchestrate long-running, asynchronous AI agent workflows with robust state management and fallback mechanisms.

2. **Inference Optimization (vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, CUDA/C++)**  
   With foundation models ubiquitous, unit economics matter. Roles requiring knowledge of KV caching strategies, quantizations (AWQ/FP4), and custom kernel optimization are seeing high inbound recruiter interest.

3. **Rust for Compute & Tooling**  
   Rust continues its standard-setter trajectory across infrastructure, database engine internals, fast WebAssembly modules, and high-throughput backend services.

4. **AI Observability & Automated Evals (Ragas, Braintrust, Phoenix)**  
   Evaluating non-deterministic agent outputs systematically—rather than relying on manual spot-checks—is now a standard hiring requirement for mid-to-senior AI roles.

5. **TypeScript + React 19 Frameworks (Next.js/Remix)**  
   Despite code generation shifts, full-stack React capabilities remain the undisputed standard for building enterprise-grade, high-interactivity user interfaces around AI agents.

## 💰 Salary Signals

* **Senior AI Systems / ML Infrastructure Engineer (US Remote / Hubs):** **$230,000 – $320,000 base** (plus equity). Specialists with low-level CUDA experience pulling up to $360k base in Series B/C startups.
* **Senior Full-Stack Engineer (AI Capabilities):** **$170,000 – $225,000 base**. Engineers demonstrating end-to-end delivery of integrated LLM features command a ~12–15% premium over traditional CRUD roles.
* **Compensation Structure Shift:** Cash base salaries remain elevated, but stock grant values are being scrutinized more closely. Startups are offering higher cash components to offset less liquid equity valuations.

---

## 🚀 Roles in High Demand

1. **AI Infrastructure & Compute Engineer**  
   *Why:* Companies need to scale self-hosted model fleets and manage GPU workloads reliably while keeping latency and cloud expenditure from exploding.

2. **Forward-Deployed AI Engineer (FDSE)**  
   *Why:* Enterprise sales depend on custom integration. Companies need developers who combine client-facing domain understanding with fast execution to build production integrations on enterprise data.

3. **Platform / Internal Developer Loop Engineers**  
   *Why:* As internal software tooling becomes heavily AI-assisted, platform teams are charged with building secure, compliant internal developer platforms (IDPs) and sandbox environments.
