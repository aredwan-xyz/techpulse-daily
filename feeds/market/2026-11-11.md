# Tech Job Market Pulse: November 11, 2026

Analyzing the latest hiring threads (including the current *Ask HN: Who is hiring?* digest), job board aggregators, and compensation disclosures. Here is your mid-November snapshot of the tech employment landscape.

---

## 🔥 Hottest Skills Right Now

1. **Agentic System Architecture (DSPy, LangGraph, Custom Runtimes)**
   Moving past basic RAG. Employers want developers who can architect stateful, multi-agent workflows with deterministic guardrails and fallback mechanisms.
2. **CUDA & Triton Kernel Tuning**
   With inference costs driving backend budgets, companies are optimizing self-hosted open-weights models (Llama 4 derivatives, Mistral variants) rather than relying solely on proprietary APIs.
3. **Rust (Systems & High-Throughput Middleware)**
   Dominating inference routers, vector databases, and real-time streaming engines. If your backend stack handles high-concurrency AI workloads, Rust experience gets immediate recruiter responses.
4. **TypeScript + Modern Web Frameworks (Next.js / Remix)**
   The product engineering stack remains firmly TypeScript-centric. Demand is highest for engineers who know how to build low-latency, streaming-first interfaces for multi-modal applications.
5. **LLM Evaluation & Automated Testing (Evals)**
   Building systematic benchmarks, regression testing for non-deterministic model outputs, and synthetic dataset curation are now baseline requirements for production AI teams.

---

## 💰 Salary Signals

* **AI Infrastructure / Inference Engineers:** US base salaries are sitting between **$240,000 – $340,000** (plus equity), up roughly ~12% year-over-year as enterprises aggressively move model hosting in-house to curb API overhead.
* **Senior Product Engineers (Full-Stack / TypeScript):** Base compensation has stabilized at **$185,000 – $235,000** for remote US roles, with top-tier Series B–D startups offering higher cash-to-equity ratios to secure senior talent.
* **Non-Engineering "Prompt Specialists":** Base bands have compressed sharply to **$85,000 – $115,000** (down >30% from early-wave peaks), as standard software engineering teams absorb prompt optimization into automated evaluation pipelines.

---

## 🚀 Roles in High Demand

1. **AI Systems / Inference Engineer**
   *Why:* The shift from cloud API calls to private, fine-tuned, low-latency micro-models has created a bottleneck for engineers who understand GPU memory management, quantization, and batching infrastructure (e.g., vLLM, TensorRT-LLM).
2. **Staff Product Engineer (AI UX / Interactive Media)**
   *Why:* Traditional web UI paradigms (forms, dashboards) are shifting toward streaming, context-aware interfaces. Companies need senior builders who bridge high-velocity frontend work with backend model pipelines.
3. **Platform & Reliability Engineer (LLMops/Edge)**
   *Why:* Vector stores, semantic caching layers, and high-concurrency streaming routes require dedicated infrastructure. Teams need SREs who understand cost-routing and GPU cluster health alongside standard Kubernetes stacks.

---

## 📉 What's Cooling Off

* **Pure "Prompt Engineers" & No-Code Glue Developers:** Roles focused solely on raw prompt crafting without underlying software architecture depth are disappearing. Logic is moving into code, compilers, and framework-level optimizers.
* **Junior CRUD Web Generalists:** Entry-level positions for basic API/frontend development continue to contract as AI-assisted coding tools raise the bar for what mid-level developers can ship solo. Niche expertise or deep systems knowledge is now required early on.

---

## 🌍 Remote Landscape

* **Mid-Sized Startups (50–500 employees):** 65%+ of listings in current hiring threads offer **fully remote** options, using location flexibility as their primary leverage to compete against Big Tech compensation packages.
* **Enterprise & Big Tech:** Standardized around strict 3-day hybrid mandates. However, specialized AI infrastructure roles are frequently granted explicit remote exceptions due to high talent scarcity.
* **Geo-Filtering:** Asynchronous work across US/EU time zones is gaining traction, with an increase in contracts explicitly looking for LATAM and Eastern European senior talent for US-based startups.
