## 🔥 Hottest Skills Right Now

1. **Agentic Workflow Orchestration (LangGraph, AutoGen, Custom Rust Runners)**  
   Companies have moved past simple RAG pipelines. The high demand is in building multi-agent systems with deterministic state machines, dynamic tool calling, and structured output guarantees.
2. **CUDA & Triton Kernel Optimization**  
   With the rollout of Blackwell architectures and custom enterprise inference chips, engineers who can squeeze 20%+ efficiency out of GPU clusters through custom Triton or CUDA kernels are commanding top-tier offers.
3. **Automated AI Evaluation Frameworks (Ragas, TruLens, Custom Evals)**  
   Shipping AI to production requires rigorous CI/CD evals. Companies are actively hiring developers who build synthetic dataset pipelines and automated output scoring to prevent regression in production agents.
4. **Rust for High-Throughput Microservices**  
   Rust is increasingly replacing Python/Node for backend API layers wrapping AI inference engines, real-time data ingestion, and edge proxying.
5. **PostgreSQL / pgvector Tuning at Scale**
