# Tech Job Market Pulse: November 9, 2026

## 🔥 Hottest Skills Right Now

1. **Agentic Workflow Orchestration (LangGraph, Temporal, Custom Control Loops)**
   Companies are moving past simple RAG pipelines to autonomous, multi-step agents. Experience with stateful orchestration, failure recovery, and tool-use verification is currently the #1 requested skill in startup job descriptions.
2. **Rust for Compute & Inference Optimization**
   With token-per-second costs driving margins, companies are rewriting critical path AI infrastructure (custom runners, vector indexers, and proxy layers) in Rust to drop latency and memory footprints.
3. **Real-time Multimodal Streaming (WebRTC + Voice/Vision Models)**
   Low-latency multimodal interactions (voice-to-voice, real-time video processing) are blowing up. Developers who know how to wire streaming backends to low-latency edge inference models are in tight supply.
4. **TypeScript / Next.js (AI-Native UX)**
   Building interface state for non-deterministic AI outputs requires complex UI patterns (generative UI, optimistic updates, streaming text parsers). Senior frontend devs who understand these patterns are seeing outsized demand.
5. **Synthetic Data & Eval Pipeline Engineering**
   As model quality plateauing is countered by specialized fine-tuning, expertise in building programmatic evaluation suites (Evals) and synthetic dataset generation pipelines is becoming a standard requirement for senior ML roles.

## 💰 Salary Signals

* **Staff AI Systems / Inference Engineer:** Base ranges have pushed to **$240,000 – $340,000** (plus substantial equity packages), driven by competition to optimize GPU cluster costs and inference speed.
* **Senior Full-Stack AI Engineer:** Base salaries have stabilized around **$180,000 – $230,000**. Candidates with demonstrated experience handling complex agent state machines command a 12–15% premium.
* **Senior MLOps / Platform Engineer:** Compensation remains strong at a median of **$215,000**, with heavy emphasis shifting away from simple model deployment toward cost governance, token observability, and fallback routing.
