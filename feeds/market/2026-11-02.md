## 🔥 Hottest Skills Right Now

1. **Agentic System Architecture & Orchestration**  
   Basic RAG is now table stakes. The real demand lies in engineers who can build multi-agent workflows with strict deterministic fallbacks, self-correction loops, and custom tool-use engines.

2. **Rust for AI Infrastructure & High-Throughput Serving**  
   With low-latency inference driving competitive advantage, Rust has become the preferred language for building GPU orchestration layers, custom model sidecars, and edge runtime environments.

3. **PostgreSQL & Advanced Vector Extensions (`pgvector`)**  
   Companies are consolidating away from standalone niche vector databases in favor of enterprise-proven Postgres setups. Deep proficiency in indexing, hybrid search, and scaling `pgvector` workloads is in high demand.

4. **Synthetic Data Pipeline Engineering**  
   As frontier models hit data scaling ceilings, enterprise teams are hiring specialized data engineers to construct clean, domain-specific synthetic generation and evaluation pipelines.

5. **TypeScript / Next.js (Server Components & Streaming UI)**  
   The full-stack web stack of choice remains TypeScript. Demand is heavily skewed toward developers skilled at handling streaming UI patterns and reactive state for non-deterministic AI outputs.
