# Tech Job Market Pulse: November 2, 2026

Analyzing today’s top hiring posts, active listings, and community signals (including the fresh **November *Ask HN: Who is hiring?*** thread), here is your start-of-month pulse on the developer market.

---

## 🔥 Hottest Skills Right Now

1. **Agentic Frameworks (LangGraph, AutoGen, CrewAI)**
   Basic RAG and prompt wrappers are table stakes. The highest volume of fresh listings demands experience building stateful, multi-agent orchestrations with explicit fallback loops and deterministic execution boundaries.

2. **Triton & CUDA Kernel Optimization**
   With inference costs still eating margins, startups and enterprise AI teams alike are desperate for low-level engineers who can write custom Triton kernels or optimize vLLM/TensorRT-LLM serving pipelines.

3. **Rust for AI Infrastructure & Tooling**
   Rust dominates the infrastructure side of modern tech stacks. From high-throughput vector routing to building native WASM execution environments for AI agents, Rust experience is fetching immediate interviews.

4. **Hybrid Search Architecture (pgvector, Qdrant, BM25 Fusion)**
   Pure semantic vector search has proven insufficient for enterprise reliability. Companies are actively hiring engineers who understand hybrid retrieval algorithms, dense/sparse embeddings, and indexing strategies at terabyte scale.

5. **TypeScript + React 19 / Server-Driven Streaming UI**
   On the product side, full-stack engineers are expected to master generative UI streams (Vercel AI SDK patterns, server actions) to render real-time structured model outputs seamlessly.

---

## 💰 Salary Signals

* **Senior/Staff AI Infrastructure Engineer (US Remote / Bay Area):** **$255,000 – $330,000 base** + significant equity. Candidates with proven experience scaling inference clusters or training pipelines are commanding top-of-band offers, up roughly 10% compared to H1 2026.
* **Senior Full-Stack Engineer (AI Product Integration):** **$170,000 – $220,000 base**. Compensation for standard web development has stabilized, but adding agent orchestration and LLM evaluation experience to a full-stack profile unlocks a ~$25k–$40k premium.
* **Fractional / Contract AI Systems Architect:** **$150 – $225/hour**. Mid-market SaaS companies retrofitting existing platforms with agentic workflows are driving high demand for 3–6 month contract engagements.

---

## 🚀 Roles in High Demand

1. **AI Application Engineer (Product Focus)**
   * **Why:** Companies need developers who can glue non-deterministic models into clean, resilient UX. The emphasis is on building reliable systems despite model latency, context limits, and halluncinations.

2. **Distributed Systems / ML Platform Engineer**
   * **Why:** Serving 70B+ parameter open-weights models locally or in private clouds requires serious systems engineering—load balancing, GPU memory management, and high-throughput streaming pipelines.

3. **Data Platform Engineer (Real-Time & Unstructured ETL)**
   * **Why:** AI models are only as good as the context fed into them. High demand exists for engineers who can ingest unstructured data (PDFs, logs, streams), extract structured metadata, and maintain low-latency RAG pipelines.

---

## 📉 What's Cooling Off

* **Non-Coding "Prompt Engineers" & Generic AI Strategists:** 
  Dedicated prompt engineering roles have virtually vanished. Prompting techniques are now expected as a standard utility tool for software engineers. If you can't write code or build systems around the prompt, the market is closed.
* **Siloed Junior CRUD Developers:** 
  Standard entry-level web dev roles remain highly contested. Automated code generation tools have shifted junior hiring expectations toward developers who possess strong system architecture fundamentals, debugging skills, and domain knowledge.

---

## 🌍 Remote Landscape

* **Startup Remote Multiplier:** ~60% of postings in today's *Ask HN: Who is hiring?* thread offer full-remote or remote-first options, primarily from Seed to Series B startups competing for specialized global talent (Rust, C++, ML Infra).
* **Enterprise Hybrid Stabilization:** Enterprise and late-stage tech companies have largely settled on rigid 3-day in-office hybrid mandates.
* **Flat Geographic Bands:** More US startups are adopting single "US National" salary bands rather than adjusting compensation downward for lower-cost-of-living regions, in an effort to secure top-tier remote talent faster.

---

## 💡 Career Move of the Week

**Build an automated Evaluation (Evals) Pipeline for your portfolio or side project.**

Hiring managers report that 90% of applicants can hook up an API call to an LLM, but less than 10% know how to programmatically evaluate accuracy, latency, and regression when models update. 

If you want to stand out in interviews this month, be ready to explain how you implement unit tests, regression suites, and observability frameworks (e.g., using open-source eval harnesses) for non-deterministic AI workflows.
