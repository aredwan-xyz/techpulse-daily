# 🚨 Tech Job Market Pulse: November 02, 2026
*Analysis based on early signals from the November "Ask HN: Who is Hiring?" thread and Q4 talent market data.*

---

## 🔥 Hottest Skills Right Now

1. **Agentic Frameworks (LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen)**  
   Companies are moving beyond simple zero-shot calls. Demand for engineers who can construct multi-agent orchestration, state persistence, and deterministic agent workflows has surged 40% year-over-year.
2. **Rust for AI Infrastructure**  
   High-performance inference engines, vLLM extensions, and custom CUDA wrappers are driving heavy demand for Rust developers who understand low-level memory layout and hardware acceleration.
3. **Small Language Model (SLM) Optimization & Quantization**  
   On-device and local enterprise models (Llama-3.x, Phi-4 derivatives) are taking over. Expertise in GGML/GGUF, AWQ, and ONNX runtime optimization for edge/private deployment is at a premium.
4. **Hybrid Search Architecture (PostgreSQL + pgvector / Qdrant)**  
   Basic RAG is dead; enterprise production RAG requires hybrid lexical/semantic search, dense-sparse reranking, and contextual filtering.
5. **TypeScript + Next.js (AI Native App Interfaces)**  
   Frontend tech stack demands have shifted toward real-time streaming UI, WebSockets/SSE for agent outputs, and fast iterative UI/UX for AI co-pilots.

## 💰 Salary Signals

* **Senior AI Infrastructure Engineers:** Base compensation is settling between **$230,000 – $310,000 USD** (US Remote/Metro), with total packages scaling past $420k for candidates with experience scaling LLM serving clusters.
* **Full-Stack Engineers (AI-integrated):** Holding strong at **$165,000 – $215,000 USD**. Meanwhile, traditional full-stack roles without AI/LLM toolchain integration see a ~5% YoY salary compression.
* **AI Quality & Evaluation Engineers (Evals/MLOps):** A rapidly emerging specialty commanding **$190,000 – $240,000 USD**, driven by the need to benchmark agent failure rates, cost-per-token efficiency, and regression testing via frameworks like DSPy and Braintrust.

## 🚀 Roles in High Demand

1. **AI Inference & Latency Engineer**  
   *Why:* As token volume scales exponentially, startups and enterprises alike are desperate to slice milliseconds and fractions of a cent off every API call.
2. **Autonomous Agent Architect**  
   *Why:* Companies in the November HN hiring thread are shifting from chat interfaces to background execution systems (coding agents, auto-reconciling accounting software, workflow automation).
3. **Product-Minded AI Generalist (Full-Stack)**  
   *Why:* Early-stage (Seed/Series A) startups are hiring versatile engineers who can design a feature, connect LLM APIs, tune context windows, and ship slick client-side interfaces without heavy overhead.

## 📉 What's Cooling Off

* **Pure Prompt Engineers:** Prompting has been largely abstracted into automated programmatic frameworks (like DSPy) or folded into standard developer workflows. Standalone prompt roles are practically nonexistent in today's postings.
* **Junior Traditional Web Developers:** Entry-level web roles focusing solely on CRUD operations, basic HTML/CSS, and standard REST APIs face extreme competition, heavily amplified by automated code generation tools.

---

## 🌍 Remote Landscape

* **Big Tech vs. Startups Split:** Big Tech continues enforcing strict 3-to-4 day mandatory office badge tracking. However, **over 62% of startup listings** in this month’s *Who is Hiring* thread remain 100% remote-friendly—using global talent pools as a leverage play against high local tier-1 tech salaries.
* **Asymmetric Competition:** Remote AI/ML roles receive 8–10x more applicants than hybrid roles. Standing out requires public code, open-source contributions, or verifiable benchmarks rather than standard resumes.
