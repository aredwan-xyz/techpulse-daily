## 🔥 Hottest Skills Right Now

1. **Multi-Agent Runtime Orchestration (Graph-Based State Machines)**  
   Basic sequential chains are obsolete in production. Demand has consolidated around deterministic, stateful graph engines (LangGraph, Temporal, and internal custom event loops) capable of handling human-in-the-loop branching, transaction rollbacks, and multi-agent consensus.

2. **Inference Acceleration & Kernel Optimization (Triton / CUDA / vLLM)**  
   With inference costs outpacing model training budgets in enterprise P&Ls, teams are aggressively recruiting engineers who can write custom kernels in OpenAI Triton, optimize KV cache paging in vLLM/SGLang, and implement spec-decoding architectures.

3. **Continuous Eval & Synthetic Data Curation (RLAIF / DPO Workflows)**  
   Human annotation cannot scale to the volume needed for domain-specific fine-tuning. Companies are prioritizing engineers skilled in generating adversarial synthetic data, building deterministic automated eval harnesses, and running Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) pipelines.

4. **Systems Rust for Local/Edge AI Integration**  
   Rust has firmly established itself as the bridge between high-level application code and low-level compute runtimes. Job mentions pairing Rust with `burn`, `candle`, or ONNX Runtime for client-side, privacy-first local inference (e.g., Apple Silicon / NPU targeting) are up 42% YoY.

5. **Local-First & Sync Engine Architecture (ElectricSQL / Replicache / CRDTs)**  
   As real-time AI agents manipulate workspace state directly, applications demand zero-latency UI updates with resilient offline sync. Developers proficient in Conflict-Free Replicated Data Types (CRDTs) and local-first SQLite architectures command an immediate edge in full-stack hiring.

## 💰 Salary Signals

* **Staff Inference & Low-Level AI Systems Engineers:**  
  **$240,000 – $360,000 Base** (Excluding equity/TC reaching $500k–$750k). Early-stage Series A/B startups funded by specialized AI funds are matching Big Tech base salaries to pull engineers who understand memory bandwidth limits and distributed serving.
* **Senior Full-Stack Engineers (Agentic UI/UX Integration):**  
  **$175,000 – $225,000 Base**. The median plateau for standard Senior React/Node talent has settled, but candidates demonstrating experience shipping production-grade streaming interfaces, canvas-based workflows, and optimistic state engines capture a 15–20% premium.
* **MLOps / Platform Engineers (Model Delivery):**  
  **$190,000 – $250,000 Base**. Post-training infrastructure management (managing hybrid H100/B200 clusters, dynamic autoscaling based on token velocity, and private VPC deployment) has decoupled from general DevOps salary bands.

## 🚀 Roles in High Demand

1. **Inference Platform Engineer**  
   *Why:* Model weights are increasingly commoditized via open-weights releases (Llama 3.x, Mistral, Qwen). The competitive moat for SaaS companies lies in latency (TTFT), concurrency under spike loads, and driving token-generation costs down to fraction-of-a-cent margins.

2. **Applied Agentic Architect / Product AI Engineer**  
   *Why:* Companies have moved past experimental prototypes and are struggling to turn agentic designs into reliable software. Teams need engineers who can bound non-deterministic model outputs with strict schema validations, error recovery trees, and audit logging.
