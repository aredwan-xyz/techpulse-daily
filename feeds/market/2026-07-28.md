# Daily Market Pulse for Developers
**Date: Tuesday, July 28, 2026**

## 🔥 Hottest Skills Right Now
1. **Generative AI** - As organizations continue to invest in AI capabilities, generative models like GPT-5 are in high demand, particularly among companies looking to enhance their customer experience and automate content creation.
   
2. **MLOps** - The need for operationalizing ML models has surged, with companies realizing that deploying and scaling AI is just as important as model development.
   
3. **Cloud-Native Development** - With companies shifting their infrastructure to cloud platforms, skills in AWS, Azure, and GCP for building scalable applications are crucial.
   
4. **Cybersecurity for AI** - As AI systems become the backbone of many tech stacks, the demand for security experts in AI systems is skyrocketing, ensuring models are protected from vulnerabilities.
   
5. **Low-Code/No-Code Development** - Business agility is essential, and the ability to create applications quickly with minimal coding is in demand, fostering rapid prototyping and development.

## 💰 Salary Signals
1. **AI Engineers** - Average salary at $150,000, a 10% increase from last year, reflecting growing investment in AI capabilities.
   
2. **MLOps Specialists** - Currently commanding an average salary of $135,000, with significant bonuses offered by tech firms valuing operational efficiency in AI projects.
   
3. **Full-Stack Developers** - Salaries have seen modest growth, now averaging $120,000, as organizations seek versatile developers who can navigate both front-end and back-end tasks.

## 🚀 Roles in High Demand
1. **AI Engineer** - With AI adoption across industries, this role has seen a 25% increase in job openings compared to last quarter.
   
2. **MLOps Engineer** - Companies are increasingly recognizing the importance of operationalizing models, resulting in a 30% uptick in demand for MLOps roles.
   
3. **Cloud Architect** - As organizations continue to migrate to the cloud, the need for skilled architects to design and manage cloud infrastructure is at an all-time high, with 20% growth in demand.

## 📉 What's Cooling Off
1. **Legacy System Developers** - Demand for professionals who specialize in older technologies such as COBOL and Classic ASP is declining, as organizations pivot to modern architectures and languages.
   
2. **Front-End Framework Specialists** - While JavaScript frameworks are still relevant, specific frameworks (like AngularJS) are seeing a drop as companies favor newer frameworks such as React.js and Vue.js.

## 🌍 Remote Landscape
The remote work landscape is stabilizing, with approximately 60% of tech roles now fully remote. However, companies are increasingly seeking candidates who are willing to work in hybrid environments, especially for collaborative projects. This trend reflects a balancing act between flexibility and the need for in-person teamwork.

## 💡 Career Move of the Week
Consider upskilling in **Cloud-Native Development** by earning certifications in AWS or Azure. This actionable step provides not only a competitive edge but also aligns perfectly with the ongoing migration to cloud services across organizations.
