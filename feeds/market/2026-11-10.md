# Tech Job Market Pulse — Tuesday, November 10, 2026

Analyzing the latest hiring patterns from the November *Ask HN: Who is hiring right now?* thread and broader market signals, today’s landscape heavily favors engineers who bridge high-performance systems engineering with autonomous AI workflows.

---

## 🔥 Hottest Skills Right Now

1. **Agentic System Architecture** — Beyond simple RAG; teams are aggressively hiring for multi-agent orchestration, state persistence, and deterministic execution guards (LangGraph, Autogen, and custom Rust-based agent loops).
2. **Rust for AI Infrastructure** — As inference costs remain a board-level metric, Rust is dominating the tooling layer, from high-performance custom vector stores to WASM-based edge inference runtimes.
3. **On-Device & Small Language Model (SLM) Optimization** — Deep experience with quantization (GGUF, AWQ), Apple MLX, and compiling sub-7B models to run locally on client devices (iOS, Android, Apple Silicon).
4. **GPU Cluster Management & Kubernetes (KubeRay, Slurm)** — Companies building proprietary models or hosting large-scale inference farms need platform engineers who can reduce GPU idle time and debug NVLink/NCCL bottlenecks.
5. **Next.js & Generative UI (TypeScript)** — Streaming canvas interfaces, real-time spatial UIs, and dynamic component rendering powered by LLM outputs have turned TypeScript/Next.js into an essential pairing for AI-native frontends.

## 💰 Salary Signals

* **AI Platform & Infrastructure Engineers:** Top-tier roles (Bay Area / US Remote) are commanding **$230k – $320k base** + equity, driven by bidding wars for engineers who understand both PyTorch internals and bare-metal cluster management.
* **Senior Full-Stack Engineers (AI-Native Focus):** Stabilized around **$175k – $240k base**. Startups are prioritizing senior generalists who can construct an entire feature end-to-end, including custom model evaluation pipelines.
* **Legacy Web/Frontend Developers (Non-AI/Ops integrated):** Compensation has plateaued at **$135k – $165k base**, with compressed equity packages compared to 2024–2025 levels.
