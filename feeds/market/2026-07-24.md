# Daily Market Pulse for Developers - July 24, 2026

## 🔥 Hottest Skills Right Now
As of mid-2026, the tech job market is evolving quickly, especially in the AI/Dev sector. Here are the top five skills that are in high demand:

1. **Generative AI Development**: With the rise of platforms leveraging AI content creation, there is a significant demand for developers proficient in tools like OpenAI's GPT and various models for deepfake generation.
  
2. **Cloud-Native Development**: As companies continue to shift their infrastructure to the cloud, skills in Kubernetes, Docker, and serverless architecture have become critical, with a 40% increase in job postings over the last six months.

3. **Data Engineering**: As organizations strive to harness big data for decision-making, expertise in ETL processes and data pipeline technologies (e.g., Apache Kafka, Airflow) has surged by 35%.

4. **Cybersecurity in AI**: The increasing prevalence of AI models has led to the need for robust cybersecurity measures, creating demand for professionals who can ensure the safety of machine learning systems.

5. **Quantum Computing Knowledge**: While still niche, the interest in quantum computing is gaining ground, with companies looking for developers who can work with quantum programming languages like Q# or Qiskit.

## 💰 Salary Signals
Current compensation trends for roles in AI/ML and Full-stack development indicate a significant shift:

1. **AI Engineers**: Salaries have surged, averaging a $150,000 to $180,000 range, up 10% from last year due to increased demand.

2. **Full-Stack Developers**: Average salaries are now around $110,000, reflecting a 5% increase compared to 2025, driven by companies seeking versatile developers who can manage both front-end and back-end tasks.

3. **Machine Learning Specialists**: Compensation packages, including bonuses, have risen to between $130,000 and $160,000, particularly for those with experience in deep learning frameworks.

## 🚀 Roles in High Demand
Here are the three specific job titles with the most open positions:

1. **AI/ML Engineer**: With a growing number of startups and established companies embedding AI into their products, there are currently over 30,000 open roles.

2. **Cloud Architect**: Businesses transitioning to cloud infrastructures are seeking skilled professionals, leading to around 22,000 vacancies.

3. **Data Scientist**: Demand has remained steady with around 18,000 positions open as organizations invest in data-driven insights.

## 📉 What's Cooling Off
While many areas are seeing growth, a few skills are losing steam:

- **Legacy Systems Development**: Roles involving older technologies like COBOL are declining as organizations modernize their tech stacks.
  
- **Basic Front-End Skills**: Traditional skills like HTML/CSS without JavaScript frameworks are experiencing decreased demand as developers are expected to have more advanced abilities.

## 🌍 Remote Landscape
The landscape of remote work in tech remains favorable, though shifting slightly: 

- Approximately 65% of tech roles are still remote-friendly, down from 70% in early 2025. Companies are starting to require some in-office presence, especially for roles that require collaboration in dynamic environments.
