### General Security Advice (NVD Outage Contingency)

While automated feeds are offline, take time to perform proactive maintenance on your repository hygiene:

1. **Audit Dependencies Manually:** Run your package manager's built-in audit tools (`npm audit`, `cargo audit`, `pip-audit`, or `govulncheck`) to catch known vulnerabilities within your local ecosystem.
2. **Review Pinning & Lockfiles:** Ensure all direct and transitive dependencies are pinned via lockfiles (`package-lock.json`, `Cargo.lock`, `requirements.txt`) to prevent unsolicited updates to compromised upstream packages.
3. **Check CI Secret Scanning:** Verify that automated secret scanners (like GitGuardian or GitHub Secret Scanning) are active on all open pull requests to block accidental commits of credentials.

---

### 🧠 Security Mindset

**Treat all input as untrusted — especially internal input.**  
Vulnerabilities often arise when developers assume data coming from microservices, internal databases, or background jobs is inherently safe. Always validate, sanitize, and strictly type inputs at boundaries, regardless of where the data originates.
