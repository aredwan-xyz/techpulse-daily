### Security Mindset Tip: **Treat All Dependencies as Untrusted Input**

When integrating a third-party library or package into your project, remember that you are inheriting its vulnerabilities, its maintenance history, and its supply chain risks. 

**Action for today:** Run a Software Composition Analysis (SCA) tool (like `npm audit`, `pip-audit`, or Dependabot) across your active repositories today. Review any high-severity alerts and ensure your lockfiles are up to date to prevent dependency confusion and supply chain attacks.
