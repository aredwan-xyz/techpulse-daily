### Proactive Security Actions for Today

While automated vulnerability feeds are offline, take 10 minutes to run manual security hygiene checks on your local projects:

1. **Run Ecosystem-Native Audits:**
   Execute your package manager’s built-in audit tool to check against cached or alternative advisory databases:
   * **Node.js:** `npm audit` / `pnpm audit`
   * **Python:** `pip-audit`
   * **Rust:** `cargo audit`
   * **Go:** `govulncheck ./...`

2. **Check Alternative Security Sources:**
   Review vendor-specific advisory pages relevant to your tech stack (e.g., [GitHub Security Advisory Database](https://github.com/advisories), [OSV.dev](https://osv.dev/), or your cloud provider's bulletin page).

3. **Prune Unused Dependencies:**
   Dead code and unused packages still add attack surface. Take time today to remove dependencies your project no longer uses.

---

### 🧠 Security Mindset

> **Practice "Least Dependency"**  
> Before adding a new third-party library to solve a small problem, ask: *Can we write this natively in 20 lines of clean code?* Every dependency you bring in becomes a permanent maintenance obligation and a potential vector for supply chain attacks. Minimize your attack surface by keeping your dependency tree as lean as possible.
