### Proactive Defense: While Upstream Feeds Are Down

When external vulnerability reporting systems experience downtime, fall back on proactive internal hygiene:

1. **Run Local Audits:** Don't rely solely on automated nightly alerts. Run package-level vulnerability checks locally or in your CI pipelines (e.g., `npm audit`, `pip-audit`, `cargo audit`, or `govulncheck`).
2. **Review Pending Dependency Updates:** If you have open Dependabot/Renovate PRs, prioritize merging security-related patch releases after standard testing.
3. **Verify Lockfiles:** Ensure your package manifests use strict version pinning and committed lockfiles (`package-lock.json`, `poetry.lock`, etc.) to prevent non-deterministic builds from pulling unvetted minor/patch updates.

---

### Security Mindset
**Never trust an automatic PR just because it's automated.**  
When reviewing automated dependency upgrade PRs (like Dependabot or Renovate), always inspect the release notes, diffs, and package maintainer changes. Malicious typosquatting or compromised maintainer accounts often disguise supply-chain attacks as benign, routine version bumps.
