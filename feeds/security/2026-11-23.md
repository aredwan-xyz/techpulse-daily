### Fallback Security Checklist for Today

While external vulnerability feeds are offline, take 10 minutes to run local automated checks on your repositories:

1. **Run Local Audits:** Execute your package manager’s built-in audit tool to catch known vulnerabilities in your dependency tree:
   * **Node.js:** `npm audit` or `pnpm audit`
   * **Python:** `pip-audit`
   * **Rust:** `cargo audit`
   * **Go:** `govulncheck ./...`
2. **Check Lockfiles:** Ensure your dependency lockfiles (`package-lock.json`, `Cargo.lock`, `requirements.txt`) are committed so build pipelines remain deterministic.
3. **Review Dependabot/Snyk PRs:** Clear out any pending security PRs sitting in your repository queue.

---

### 💡 Security Mindset
**Practice Defense-in-Depth at the Boundary:** Never assume internal APIs, database entries, or microservice payloads are safe just because they are "inside the perimeter." Treat *all* incoming data as untrusted, and validate/sanitize it at the exact point of entry into your component.
