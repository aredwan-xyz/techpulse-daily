### Security Actions for Today

While the primary feed is down, rely on local tooling and alternative databases to maintain your project's security posture:

1. **Check Alternative Sources:** Use the [GitHub Advisory Database](https://github.com/advisories) or [OSV.dev](https://osv.dev/) to check for open-source vulnerability updates relevant to your stack.
2. **Run Local Audits:** Execute your package manager's native audit tool (`npm audit`, `cargo audit`, `pip-audit`, `govulncheck`, etc.) against your codebase. These draw from multi-source databases and do not rely solely on the NVD.
3. **Review Pending Security PRs:** Check for open Dependabot or Renovate PRs in your repositories and merge patch-level updates for low-risk dependencies.

---

### 💡 Security Mindset
**Practice "Least Dependency":** Before running `npm install` or adding a package to your manifest, ask if the functionality can be implemented cleanly with standard language features. Every third-party library introduced adds code you didn't write to your attack surface and creates another maintenance burden for your future self.
