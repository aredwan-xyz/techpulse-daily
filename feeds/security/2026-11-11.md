### General Security Advice: Handling Feed Disruptions & Dependency Health

When vulnerability feeds go down, fall back on proactive defensive measures:

1. **Run Local Dependency Audits:** 
   Don't rely solely on centralized feeds. Use ecosystem-native tools locally and in CI/CD pipelines to catch known vulnerabilities:
   * **Node.js:** `npm audit` or `pnpm audit`
   * **Python:** `pip-audit`
   * **Rust:** `cargo audit`
   * **Go:** `govulncheck ./...`
2. **Review Automated Scanner Health:** 
   Verify that your secondary security scanners (e.g., Dependabot, Snyk, Trivy, or Socket) are actively running on your main branches and PRs.
3. **Audit High-Risk Dependencies:** 
   Check your project's top-level dependencies—especially web frameworks, authentication libraries, and serialization engines—to ensure you are running the latest stable patch versions.
