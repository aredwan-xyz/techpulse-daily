## 📝 Original Abstract

> Symbolic knowledge (motif counts, ring counts, label constraints) is routinely added to graph neural networks (GNNs) and justified by their limited expressivity. We replace this yardstick with an exact one. Rule design thus becomes rule discovery: we score candidates on the network's out-of-fold residual against calibrated permutation nulls with family-wise error control.
