## 🎯 The Core Idea
WebHarbor is a suite of 153 Dockerized, fully functional web app environments designed to train and test AI web agents reliably. Think of it as a self-hosted, offline snapshot of real-world websites that run locally under full developer control.

## 📝 Original Abstract

> GUI web agents require realistic and interactive environments that remain stable during both evaluation and training. Live website evaluation suffers from changing content, access restrictions, and limited coverage of authenticated workflows, while existing synthetic environments for training often lack functional depth and visual fidelity. We propose WebHarbor, a suite of 153 executable and dockerized website environments that faithfully reconstruct the visual interfaces, content, and functional workflows of real-world web services under deterministic control.
