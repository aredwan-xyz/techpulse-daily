## 🎯 The Core Idea (2 sentences)
Instead of building complex 3D internal models, this system answers questions about 3D scenes by smartly picking the most helpful 2D camera angles. It works completely out-of-the-box without requiring any expensive model training.

## 🔍 The Problem They Solved (2 sentences)
Traditional 3D scene understanding relies on heavy data structures like point clouds or voxels, which are computationally expensive and hard to scale. Alternatively, training massive multi-modal AI models for 3D is data-hungry and often unnecessary for answering specific visual questions.

## 💡 The Key Innovation (2 sentences)
The pipeline breaks down a user's question into distinct conceptual attributes needed for reasoning. It then uses a coarse-to-fine search process to dynamically find and score the exact subset of 2D images that contain the visual evidence to answer the query.

## 🛠️ Why Developers Should Care (2 sentences)
You can build powerful 3D spatial reasoning apps using standard 2D cameras and off-the-shelf vision models without touching complex 3D rendering or training. It saves massive amounts of compute and lets you leverage existing 2D foundation models for 3D tasks.

## ⚡ TL;DR
Instead of teaching AI to see in 3D, this system just figures out which 2D photos give the best angle to answer a question.

> 3D scene understanding tasks are often approached by building explicit 3D scene representations or by training specialized multi-modal models over point clouds, voxels, or scene graphs. We present a training-free, query-conditioned multi-view pipeline that performs diverse tasks using only 2D images and a set of tools used purely for inference.
