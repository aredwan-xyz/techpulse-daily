## 🎯 The Core Idea
This research compares different ways to control residential energy devices—like batteries and heat pumps—using simple hardcoded rules, classical math optimization, and machine learning. To get the best of both worlds, the author introduces **TreeC**, a new machine learning method that automatically learns smart energy strategies and outputs them as clear, human-readable rules.

## 🔍 The Problem They Solved
Traditional rule-based systems for home energy are easy to understand but waste green energy because they can't adapt to complex weather or pricing patterns. On the flip side, advanced machine learning models can optimize energy usage effectively, but they act as unpredictable "black boxes" that are difficult for engineers to trust or debug on physical hardware. 

## 💡 The Key Innovation
The thesis introduces **TreeC**, an AI approach that trains on energy data but translates its learned strategies into readable decision trees. By testing TreeC across three simulated environments and a physical, real-world setup, the research proves that you don't have to sacrifice interpretability to get high-performance energy efficiency. It bridges the gap between complex optimization algorithms and straightforward, inspectable logic.
