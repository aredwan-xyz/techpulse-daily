## 📝 Original Abstract

> State space models (SSMs) are an emerging alternative to Transformers. SSMs offer linear-time inference and parallel training, but most implementations make the parallel scan efficient by making the recurrence diagonal: each channel of the hidden state is multiplied by an input-dependent real or complex number. These numbers commute, so the state records which inputs occurred but not their order. We formalize this with representation theory, which explains and fixes the state-tracking failures previously reported for SSMs. The state update defines a linear representation of the monoid of input sequences, and we show that an SSM tracks a group at every sequence length exactly when that group is a quotient of the group generated by its transition matrices.
