## 📝 Original Abstract

> Neural circuits must maintain functionality to drive effective behavior while their size, topology, geometry, and intrinsic dynamics change across development. To model developing visuomotor circuits and behavior, we investigated whether a single compact generative encoding could produce functional recurrent controllers across such changes. Building on the HyperNEAT algorithm, we represented this program as a compositional pattern-producing network that received developmental time as an input to generate age-specific connectivity. The developmental substrate prescribed neuronal birth, position, sensory organization, and intrinsic time constants.
