## 📝 Original Abstract

> Recent work shows that large language models (LLMs) can operate directly on the parameters of other neural networks. They can read what a network was trained to do, fix a network that fails, and write working networks from scratch, all without gradient descent. This capability has been termed Weight-Space Programming (WSP). Exploring this new paradigm systematically requires a robust benchmark. We introduce WEIGHTBENCH, a benchmark that evaluates LLMs of increasing capability on 42 WSP tasks across five tracks: reading, fixing and writing weights, recognizing edits that are impossible, and data-free compression.
