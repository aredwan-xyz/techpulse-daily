## 🎯 The Core Idea
The authors created PixVid, a method that adapts existing video AI models to generate raw pixel frames directly instead of using heavily compressed "latent" representations. By replacing the standard image decoder with a lightweight rendering head, the model outputs uncompressed RGB frames frame-by-frame.

## 🔍 The Problem They Solved
Most video AI models compress frames into multi-frame "latent blocks" to save compute, but this creates massive latency for interactive streaming because you have to wait for an entire block to finish. This compression also makes it almost impossible to control individual frames or scale up to ultra-high resolutions without exploding memory usage.

## 💡 The Key Innovation
Instead of spending millions to train a pixel-based model from scratch, PixVid takes a pre-trained latent video model, strips out its VAE decoder, and attaches a simple convolutional head. The crucial trick to making this work was altering how the model learns: they forced it to predict clean, fully-rendered video frames directly instead of predicting noise or movement velocity, while tweaking the noise schedules for stability.

## 📝 Original Abstract

> Frontier video generation models work in a VAE latent space that compresses video in both space and time. We present PixVid, a simple yet effective method that lifts a pretrained latent video model to directly generate every frame in pixel space. PixVid keeps the diffusion transformer and replaces the VAE decoder with a convolutional head that renders its outputs into pixels.
