# 🔥 GitHub Trending — Wednesday, November 18 2026

> *Daily snapshot by [TechPulse Daily Digest](https://github.com/aredwan-xyz/devpulse-daily)*

## 🧠 Analyst Take

Today’s trending repositories reveal a developer ecosystem obsessed with reverse-engineering, de-bloating, and domesticating AI-generated chaos into precision tools. 

The standout project is `heydamir/unslop`, an ingenious Claude skill that aggressively strips "vibecoded" AI genericism out of user interfaces and replaces it with actual design. It’s brilliant because it weaponizes LLMs against their own worst aesthetic tendencies, marking the exact moment developers got tired of cleaning up their own AI's mess. 

This suggests the next bottleneck in software isn't generation, but curation—we are entering an era where the most valuable developer tooling acts as a high-taste filter for machine-produced sprawl.
