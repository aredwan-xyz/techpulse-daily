# 🔥 GitHub Trending — Sunday, November 22 2026

> *Daily snapshot by [TechPulse Daily Digest](https://github.com/aredwan-xyz/devpulse-daily)*

## 🧠 Analyst Take

Today's trending repositories are dominated by autonomous reverse-engineering agents and edge-harnessing tools designed to dissect, decompile, and reconstruct everything from legacy binaries to AI-generated interfaces. 

The standout project is *ItsNotRexed/Leonida*, a native PC recompilation of the Xbox version of GTA 6, which pushes the boundaries of clean-room reverse engineering into unreleased, next-generation AAA territory. 

This suggests that developer workflows are shifting from passive consumption and AI generation to aggressive, automated deconstruction, where humans use agentic tooling to tear down proprietary software and rebuild it on their own terms.
