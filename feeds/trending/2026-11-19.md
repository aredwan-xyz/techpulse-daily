# 🔥 GitHub Trending — Thursday, November 19 2026

> *Daily snapshot by [TechPulse Daily Digest](https://github.com/aredwan-xyz/devpulse-daily)*

## 🧠 Analyst Take

Today's trending repositories are defined by a relentless push to reverse-engineer, decompile, and control software—from AI-generated video and vibecoded UIs down to proprietary systems and unreleased hardware. 

The standout project is `storytold/craft-anything`, because it moves past passive AI assistance to give developers a Rust-powered blueprint for dissecting and cloning *any* piece of software on earth. 

This suggests the developer ecosystem is moving into an aggressive "post-abstraction" era, where humans refuse to accept black-box code, AI slop, or closed platforms, and are building native toolchains to crack open and rebuild the digital stack.
