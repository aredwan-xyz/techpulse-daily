Today's trending repositories show developers weaponizing Rust and AI agents to reverse-engineer, de-obfuscate, and rebuild complex systems from the ground up.

This suggests the next bottleneck in software isn't generating code, but cleaning up the massive wave of generic AI slop currently flooding every digital medium.
