# 🔥 GitHub Trending — Monday, November 16 2026

> *Daily snapshot by [TechPulse Daily Digest](https://github.com/aredwan-xyz/devpulse-daily)*

## 🧠 Analyst Take

Today’s trending repositories reflect a developer ecosystem aggressively retrofitting, reverse-engineering, and reclaiming control from closed AI models, crippled hardware limits, and bloatware. 

The standout project is **Zagitta/gpu-unlock**, because it bypasses artificial consumer-grade silicon throttling to double FP16/BF16 tensor throughput on older GeForce cards via raw C. It’s a brilliant middle finger to artificial market segmentation that immediately democratizes local AI training hardware. 

This suggests the next wave of developer tooling won't be about building new wrappers, but aggressively unlocking the latent, gatekept capacity of the hardware and software we already own.
