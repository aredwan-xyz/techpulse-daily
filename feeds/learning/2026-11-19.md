RESOURCE_TITLE: Building and Scaling Discord to Millions of Concurrent Users
RESOURCE_URL: https://discord.com/blog/how-discord-scaled-rust-to-process-trillions-of-messages-in-the-concurrency-frontier
RESOURCE_SOURCE: Discord Engineering Blog

WHY_NOW:
As real-time communication platforms handle increasingly massive concurrency frontiers, architectural decisions made around memory management and backend language shifts dictate performance. This deep-dive captures modern realities of handling trillions of messages under strict latency constraints.

WHAT_YOULL_LEARN:
- How to transition core microservices from Go to Rust to eliminate garbage collection pauses at scale
- Strategies for partitioning and sharding massive databases to handle billions of daily writes
- Techniques for optimizing low-latency WebSocket connections for millions of active concurrent users
- Real-world trade-offs of monolithic vs. distributed state management in real-time systems

WHO_ITS_FOR:
Backend engineers and system architects designing real-time, high-throughput distributed systems. It provides immense value to anyone tackling horizontal scalability and latency bottlenecks.

TIME_INVESTMENT: 35 minutes

BEST_QUOTE_OR_HIGHLIGHT:
"By moving our read states and gateway processing to Rust, we eliminated unpredictable GC tail latencies, ensuring our P99 remains rock-solid even during traffic spikes of tens of millions of concurrent connections."

NEXT_STEP:
Audit your current microservices architecture for garbage collection or thread-contention bottlenecks, and map out a performance profiling session for your critical path network boundary.
